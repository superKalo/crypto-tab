Feedback privacy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

What you choose to share

This optional form helps the Crypto Tab maintainer understand why people uninstall the extension. When you press Send feedback, we receive your selected reason and any details you choose to enter. Your email address is optional and is only included if you select “I'm open to a reply” and enter an address. We use it only to respond to your feedback.

Extension context

After an uninstall, your browser may open this page with the extension version and distribution family (Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons) after the # in the URL. This part stays in your browser until you submit feedback; it is not sent in the initial website request. Other Chromium browsers use the Chrome Web Store label too. These values are included with feedback when present. The URL does not contain a unique installation ID or your saved preferences.

How feedback reaches us

Feedback is submitted over HTTPS to Web3Forms, which processes it and sends it to the maintainer's email inbox. Web3Forms and its infrastructure and spam protection providers may process submission data and connection information, including your IP address. Processing may take place outside your country. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy for its data handling and retention practices.

This page does not send form answers until you submit them and does not add analytics or tracking cookies. The website host receives ordinary web request information, including your IP address and requested URL, and may retain technical logs. Feedback is voluntary; you can close the page without submitting it.

Use, retention, and contact

The Crypto Tab maintainer uses feedback to improve the product and, when requested, respond to you. Submitted feedback may be retained in the maintainer's inbox while reviewing product issues or handling follow-up. It is not used to subscribe you to marketing emails.

To ask about or request deletion of feedback you submitted, contact superkalo@goodmorning.dev. Include enough context to help identify the submission.
