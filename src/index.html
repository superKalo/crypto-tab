About Crypto Tab

Back in 2017, two friends got curious about Bitcoin.
Crypto Tab came out of a simple wish - open a new tab, check the price, and get on with the day.

It’s made for vibe investors, so the price is averaged by the hour, day, week or month.

First shipped in October 2017, maintained on and off ever since, and somehow grew organically to thousands of active installs! Now with dark mode, custom colors and finally an option to switch to Ether.
