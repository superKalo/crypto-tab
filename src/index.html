About Crypto Tab

Back in 2017, two friends got curious about Bitcoin.
Crypto Tab came out of a simple wish - open a new tab, check the price, and get on with the day.

Why we use averages

It’s made for vibe investors who want a feel for the bigger picture. Averaging prices over a day or month softens brief spikes and dips. That makes price trends easier to follow at a glance.

The longer views show daily averages (1W/1M) or monthly averages (1Y/ALL). Their highlighted highs and lows are averages too, so they can differ from an asset’s recorded price extremes.
